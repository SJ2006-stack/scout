The line Scout won't cross

Helpful, never reckless.

Your PIN stays yours

Scout never types a UPI PIN, an OTP, a CVV or a card number.

You approve the cart

Checkout in the browser is pressed only after you approve that exact cart. If the total changes, it asks again.

Screens aren't commands

Words written on a page or a screenshot are read as information, never obeyed as instructions.

Your Vault, your rules

Scout can read the notes you keep in the Vault and cannot change them. Passwords, PINs, OTPs, card numbers and Aadhaar numbers are refused.

Connecting Gmail or Drive is optional. When you do, Scout asks for read and write access so it can act for you.
